### Small application

The class will be divided in groups of 4 or 5 students.

1. Choose one of the following sample requirement specifications for review:
    - A currency exchange converter
    - A horoscope app
    - A measure converter
    - A sound meter
    - A mobile lantern
    - A stopwatch
    - A quote-of-the-day app
    - A metronome
    - An audio recorder
    - A notes app
2. Before reviewing, define roles within the group (the moderator and the scribe can also be reviewers):
    - 1 moderator
    - N reviewers
    - 1 scribe
3. Initiate the review process by distributing the requirement specification among the reviewers
4. Run a review meeting and create the review report
5. Upload the review report (pdf file) to Teams (folder *Review*), with the following requirements
    - The name of the file will be the name of the application plus the names of the group members
    - The file will include:
      - Name of the application
      - Date, time, and duration of the review meeting
      - Names and roles of the group members
      - List of issues found. Choose the most appropriate format

### Startup
You, a group of students from EK, have received a formal request from Copenhagen’s EK Ølbar to develop a promotional website. For this reason, you decide to create a software development startup and achieve all tasks (design, coding, testing, documentation, domain registration, server subscription and maintenance). The requirements specification is the following:
- The web domain will be www.ekbeerbar.dk
- It will consist of the following web pages:
  - Home page. Including a promotional text, some photographs, and a brief history
  - Beer list. Including prices and separating bottled beer from beer on tap
  - Events. Concerts, magicians, and poetry readings take place every Friday
  - Contact. Phone number, e-mail address, postal address, and location map
You agree with the customer on having the web site active by January 1st, 2027.

**Exercise**
Work in groups of 4 or 5.
- Define what each probability and impact mean
- Identify every possible risk
- For each risk
  - Provide an ID, a name, and a description
  - Estimate its probability and impact
  - Calculate its risk factor
  - Identify who is responsible for handling the risk
  - Elaborate a list of possible mitigation actions
  - Establish status date and follow-up date
  - Choose a status
- Elaborate the corresponding risk table
- Elaborate the corresponding risk matrix
- Upload them in a pdf, xlsx or docx document to Teams (folder *Risk Analysis Exercise*)
